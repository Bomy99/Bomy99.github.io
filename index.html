Bomy99

Premium game assets and small games, made by an independent studio.

We build the assets we wish we could buy: ornate card frames and UI kits, Roman medals and ranks, card and combat effects, and cute low-poly 3D hex kits for strategy and city builders. Every pack is made with our own Blender and code pipeline, finished by hand, and ships with a demo, documentation and several resolutions.
